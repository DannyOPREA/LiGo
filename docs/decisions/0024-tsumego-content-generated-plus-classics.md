# 0024. Tsumego content: puzzles we generate and check ourselves, plus a small classics tail
- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-09-29
- Decided by: Claude, under the owner's 2026-09-28 delegation ("Don't ask for my approval for anything"), taking the memo's own recommendation. The owner may overrule it.

## Context
Phase 8 needs at least 200 Go problems with solution trees, a difficulty spread that suits kyu
players, and a provenance record on every puzzle (PLAN §3.1, §5 unit 8.1, §8). PLAN §2.2 and
COPYING.md reject non-commercial and unclear licences, and §3.1 and §8 rule out problems from
modern books. Unit 8.1's [memo](../build-vs-buy/tsumego-content.md) checked every source it could reach:

- OGS, goproblems.com, tsumego-hero, the tasuki collections and sanderland/tsumego's data have no
  licence that lets LiGo use them (OGS's terms grant a licence to OGS only; sanderland's MIT covers
  its code, and its data comes from modern books).
- gogameguru/go-problems (422 problems with solution trees) is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, which the
  non-commercial rule rejects.
- No CC0 or CC-BY tsumego dataset was found.
- The public-domain classics (*Xuanxuan Qijing*, *Igo Hatsuyōron*, *Gokyō Shumyō*) are mostly
  dan-level. They survive as positions only, so solutions must come from the original editions.
  Their scans are on hosts the cloud can't reach, and a scan's rights line has not been read yet.

## Decision
Option D of the memo:
1. **The bulk of the set is generated by LiGo.** A generator in `tools/puzzles` builds
   life-and-death positions of its own from a catalogue of textbook eye shapes, in the corner, on
   the edge and in the centre, with outside stones varied for difficulty. It solves each position
   by exhaustive search of the small region with goban-engine's checked play (ADR 0014), gets a
   second opinion from KataGo (ADR 0016's engine), and keeps only puzzles where both agree. The
   solution tree (right answers, refutations, ko marked) comes from the search.
2. **A small dan-level tail** (about 20 to 40 problems) is transcribed by LiGo from an original
   edition of *Gokyō Shumyō*. This happens only once the scan host is reachable and its rights line
   has been read and recorded. It does not block the 200.
3. **Every puzzle records its provenance.** A generated puzzle records the generator version, its
   seed, and the KataGo version, network name and sha256. A transcribed puzzle records the work,
   edition, library ID, problem number, scan URL and page, the scan's rights line, the transcriber
   and the checker. LiGo's own puzzle files are CC0 (the generator's code is MIT, ADR 0007).
4. **No other collection is used, not even for comparison.** That covers gogameguru, OGS,
   goproblems, tsumego-hero, tasuki, sanderland and Sensei's Library, unless the owner changes the
   non-commercial rule.

## Consequences
- The generator and checker are custom code, more than glue (rung 6 of the reuse ladder). They stay
  small, reuse goban-engine for every rule, and are specified in unit 8.2's design ADR and built in
  8.3.
- Generated puzzles teach eye shapes and life and death well, but they are more uniform than
  book problems. Tesuji variety comes later, if ever, from the classics tail.
- The cloud's KataGo test network is weak, so trust comes mainly from the exact search. A stronger
  network on the owner's machine (unit 4.6's pin) makes the second opinion stronger. Before any
  public use of the set, the owner reads the licence of the network that was used (katagotraining.org).
- The owner can overrule this. The runner-up is gogameguru's set as a separate CC BY-NC-SA data
  pack, which would need the non-commercial rule rewritten.
