# What to tell each participant

Read or paraphrase this before starting, and only go ahead if they agree.

> Thanks for helping. I'm comparing two Go server lobbies, OGS and a prototype called LiGo, to see
> which is easier to use. **I'm testing the lobbies, not you**: if something is hard, that's what
> I want to find out. I'll ask you to do four small tasks on each site and to think aloud while
> you do them: say what you're looking at, what you expect and what surprises you. I won't help
> during a task, but you can give up on any task at any time, and stop the whole session whenever
> you like. It takes about 40 minutes.
>
> I'll take written notes. They name you only as a number (P1, P2, …), they don't include your
> name, contact details or usernames, and they may be published in the project's repository (and
> shared with the OGS developers). [Only if you want to record:] I'd also like to record the
> screen and audio, only so I can check my notes; I'll delete the recording once the notes are
> written. Is that OK?
>
> Any questions before we start?

Tick in the notes: agreed to take part (yes/no), agreed to a recording (yes/no/not asked).
If they don't agree to a recording, don't record.
