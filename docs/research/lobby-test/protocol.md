# Session protocol

About 40 minutes per participant: 5 set-up, 15 per site, 5 closing. Follow the wording of the
tasks exactly, so every participant hears the same thing. Keep one notes file open
([notes-template.md](notes-template.md)).

## 1. Set-up (5 min)

1. Go through [consent.md](consent.md). Stop if they don't agree.
2. Ask their rank and how often they play online, and where. Note it.
3. Explain thinking aloud once: "Say what you're looking at and what you expect, as if you were
   talking to yourself. If you go quiet I'll just say 'what are you thinking?'."
4. Open the first site on its **home page**, signed in to the participant's account (OGS: their
   own; LiGo: the account you made for them). Close anything else.

## 2. The four tasks (about 15 min per site)

Read each task aloud, say "go", start the timer. Don't help. If they are stuck for **2 minutes**,
ask "what would you try next?"; at **4 minutes**, stop the task and note "gave up". After each task
ask: **"On a scale from 1 (very difficult) to 7 (very easy), how easy was that?"** and note it.
Cancel or abandon whatever game the task started before the next task (an abort, a cancelled
challenge or seek). Play no real game.

**Task 1: quick game at your level.**
> "You have about 10 minutes and want a 19×19 game against someone around your strength, as quickly
> as possible. Get a game started."

Done when a game is found, or when they're clearly waiting in a search that would give them that
game (note which). For LiGo, your **helper** account waits in the "10 min + 5×30 s" pool on your
second device just before you say "go", so a pairing can happen; note the time to the first board
shown. On OGS, real players may or may not be there; note if they had to wait.

**Task 2: join an open game that suits you.**
> "Now look at the games other people have posted and are waiting for an opponent. Pick one that
> would suit you and join it. Tell me why you picked it."

Before you say "go" on LiGo, have your helper account post two open games: one that suits the
participant (19×19, near their rank) and one that doesn't (for example rated with a rank range that
excludes them). Done when they join one. Note which one and why; note whether they understood why
anything was greyed out or missing.

**Task 3: create a custom game.**
> "Create a game with these settings and post it for anyone to join: 19×19, Chinese rules, byo-yomi
> with 10 minutes main time and five 30-second periods, not rated, even game."

Done when the game is posted with every setting right. Note wrong settings and where they looked.

**Task 4: challenge a named player.**
> "Your friend's username is [the named-player account on LiGo; on OGS, a friend or your own OGS
> account]. Send them a challenge for a 9×9 game."

Done when the challenge is sent. Note how they found the player (search, URL, list).

Then switch to the other site, open its home page, and do the four tasks again in the same order.

## 3. Closing (5 min)

Ask the closing questions in the notes template, in order. Thank them. If you recorded, delete the
recording once your notes are complete.

## Things to watch for (don't prompt them)

- Where their eyes and mouse go first on each home page.
- Whether they can tell which open games they can join, and why others are greyed or missing.
- Whether rank labels and the widening range while waiting make sense to them.
- Hesitation over time-control wording (byo-yomi periods, Fischer).
- Anything they call "confusing", "where is…", or "I expected…".
