# Phase 9 demo: the polish, and your Go-club demo

Phase 9's acceptance (docs/PLAN.md §5, unit 9.10): **9.5's budget and 9.4's accessibility check pass
over every page, the app installs on a phone, and you have a checklist for showing LiGo at your Go
club.**

CI checks the first part already:

- **The size budget.** The `ui` job measures every page's JavaScript and CSS, plus the board, against
  `dev/ci/budget.json` on every pull request that touches lila's ui. The pages are the lobby, the
  game, the analysis board, the puzzle trainer and its dashboard, the profile, the account pages, the
  challenge page and the playground. The board's mount time is checked in Chromium too.
  `dev/ligo test budget` runs the same checks on your box.
- **Accessibility.** `lila/tests/e2e-demo/phase9-demo.spec.ts` opens every page on the real stack, as a
  guest and signed in, at desktop and phone size. It checks each page with axe for serious or critical
  WCAG 2.2 AA problems. It runs in the `e2e` workflow and in `dev/ligo e2e demo`. The keyboard play
  itself is checked on the playground and the game page by the `ui` job.

What's left for your hands: sections 1 and 2 below (about 20 minutes), then section 3 at the club.

## 1. Before the club night (Fedora box)

```sh
cd ~/"VScode Projects/LiGo"
git pull
dev/ligo up
dev/ligo status
```

- [ ] `dev/ligo status` shows lila, lila-ws, the scoring worker, Mongo and Redis up, and the UI fresh.
- [ ] http://localhost:8080 shows the lobby. Sign in with two accounts in two browsers (or a normal
      and a private window). A new local account needs its email confirmed. The site sends no mail,
      so find the link with `dev/ligo logs lila`.
- [ ] Play a 9×9 game between them to two passes, and both accept the count. The result shows on
      both.
- [ ] /training gives a puzzle. If it says there are none, run `dev/ligo puzzles load`, then wait
      up to 5 minutes or restart lila.
- [ ] Optional: download the demo video, in case the Wi-Fi at the club fails. It is the
      `ligo-demo-video` artifact of the latest `e2e` run (GitHub, Actions tab, e2e). Copy both
      `.webm` files to your laptop and check they play.

## 2. Your phone

Use the phone steps of docs/demos/phase-2.md §4 to reach the site from the phone: the same Wi-Fi,
`LILA_DOMAIN` and `LILA_URL`, and port 8080 open in the firewall.

- [ ] **Install the app.** On Android Chrome, open the menu and choose "Install app" (or "Add to
      Home screen"). On an iPhone, use Safari's Share button and then "Add to Home Screen". A LiGo icon
      appears on the home screen and opens the site without the browser's address bar.
      Chrome only offers the install on https or on localhost. If it isn't offered over the
      network, the "Add to Home screen" shortcut still works, and CI checks that the site is
      installable (`ui/playground/e2e/pwa.spec.ts`).
- [ ] **Offline page.** In the installed app, turn on airplane mode and reload: the page says "You
      are offline". Turn airplane mode off and reload: the site is back.
- [ ] **Phone play.** Play a few moves of a game on the phone against your desktop browser. A tap
      shows a preview stone, and Confirm move plays it.
- [ ] **Sounds.** Speakers on: a stone makes a sound. In a byo-yomi game, the last 10 seconds of a
      period count down out loud.

## 3. At the Go club: what to show, in order

About 15 minutes. Bring the laptop running the site, your phone, and the downloaded video as a
fallback.

1. **The lobby (2 min).** Open http://localhost:8080 on the laptop. Show the quick-pairing tiles: one click
   finds a game. Open the Custom tile to show board size, rules (Japanese or Chinese), komi, and the
   clocks: byo-yomi, Fischer, correspondence.
2. **A real game (5 min).** Ask a club member to join from your phone, or from the laptop's second
   browser. Play a 9×9 game with a capture, then both pass.
3. **The count (2 min).** KataGo proposes the dead stones. Tap a stone to change its mark and show
   that both boards update. Resume the game if anyone disagrees, then both accept. Then show the
   result.
4. **Ranks (1 min).** Open your profile: the rank shows as kyu/dan, and rated games against a
   stronger or weaker player get handicap automatically.
5. **Analysis and SGF (2 min).** Open the finished game's analysis board and step through it with the arrow keys.
   Show /paste: paste a game record from someone's own SGF file and open its analysis board.
6. **Puzzles (2 min).** Open /training and let someone solve one. Say that every puzzle was generated by
   LiGo and checked with KataGo.
7. **The polish (1 min).** In the account menu, show the board and stone themes. Show the phone app on
   your home screen.

Close with what LiGo is: a non-commercial proof of concept, built from lichess, OGS's board,
PlayStrategy's rules and KataGo. It isn't a public site.

## 4. If something breaks during the demo

| What happens | What to do |
|---|---|
| The page doesn't load, or says it can't connect | Run `dev/ligo status` in a terminal. If something is down, run `dev/ligo down` and then `dev/ligo up`. |
| The phone can't reach the laptop | Check both are on the same Wi-Fi and the firewall still allows port 8080 (phase-2.md §4). Fallback: play on the laptop with two browser windows. |
| A move doesn't appear on the other board | Reload the page; the game comes back where it was. If it keeps happening, `dev/ligo logs ws`. |
| The scoring phase marks nothing dead | KataGo isn't running, so the proposal is empty. Tap the dead stones by hand. Both players see the marks, and the count is still right. |
| /training has no puzzle | `dev/ligo puzzles load`, then restart lila (`dev/ligo down && dev/ligo up`). Meanwhile, show the analysis board. |
| No sound | The browser blocks sound until the first click or tap on the page. Click anywhere, then play a stone. |
| Anything else | Play the demo video and carry on talking. Note what broke and tell Claude afterwards; a bug report is a request for the fix. |
